### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Who is this app for?**  
GamePlan is designed for youth and adult soccer teams to help coaches create fair, strategic lineups and manage substitutions easily.

**Q: Does the app store or share my data?**  
No. GamePlan does not require accounts and does not collect or sell personal data.

**Q: Can I suggest features or improvements?**  
Absolutely! We welcome feedback and feature requests.
