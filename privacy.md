# Privacy Policy

**GamePlan Soccer Lineups** respects your privacy.

This app does **not** collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data.  
All data entered into the app remains on the user’s device unless the user chooses to share it manually.

### Information Collection
GamePlan does not require user accounts and does not collect:
- Personal information
- Location data
- Contact information
- Analytics or tracking data

### Data Sharing
The app does not share any user data with third parties.

### User-Controlled Sharing
Any sharing (such as exporting or sharing lineups) is initiated manually by the user using their device’s built-in sharing options.

### Children’s Privacy
GamePlan does not knowingly collect data from children. All usage data remains local to the device.

### Changes
This policy may be updated if the app’s functionality changes. Updates will be reflected on this page.
